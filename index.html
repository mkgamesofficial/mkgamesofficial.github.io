Block puzzle

Paradox Block

A relaxing block puzzle full of bright, candy-coloured blocks. Place them on the 8×8 board, blast full rows and columns, and multiply your score.

  • Chain combos. Keep clearing to push your multiplier to x1.5, x2.0, x2.5 and beyond.
  • Rewind time. Made a mistake? Undo your last moves and earn a CHRONO bonus.
  • Swap pieces. Got a block that won’t fit? Trade it for a better one.
  • Daily quests and free rewards. New goals every day and a free reward on the main menu.
  • Leaderboard. Post your best score on Google Play Games and compete with the world.
  • Plays offline
  • 5 languages
  • No timer
  • Continue anytime

CyberOrb

An endless track across the night sky, one ball, and a speed that never stops climbing.

One-tap arcade

MagneGap

A magnetic ball races between the N ceiling and the S floor. Every tap flips its polarity and launches it to the opposite rail.

  • One tap, endless thrills. Easy to learn, and every tap counts as the speed climbs.
  • Dodge everything. Electric spikes, swinging mines, spinning rotors and rail lasers.
  • New zones. Push your score higher for fresh colours and faster speeds.
  • 16 unique balls. Collect gold to unlock Neon Core, Saturn, Magma, Black Hole and more.
  • Plays offline
  • English and Turkish
  • Quick tutorial
